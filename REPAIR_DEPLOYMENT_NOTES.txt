IMPORTANT BEFORE GO-LIVE:
1. Upload the entire folder preserving its directory structure; do not upload index.php alone.
2. Confirm PHP 8.x is enabled and cURL is available (or allow_url_fopen is enabled for the UID lookup proxy).
3. Confirm api/products_data.json, webinfo.json, and the site root are writable only where required by your host. orders.json must be writable by PHP for order creation.
4. Change the default admin password immediately. The current admin.php contains a default credential; do not leave it on a public production site.
5. UID lookup depends on third-party endpoints in api/verify.php. If those endpoints are down or change their response schema, lookup will fail; configure a trusted authorized provider.
6. Payment is NOT automatically verified by a QR image or a user-entered UTR. Configure an official payment gateway/provider API and signed server-side webhook before fulfilling orders or marking them paid. Never treat a screenshot/typed UTR as proof of payment.
7. Product prices are the values in api/products_data.json, not live market rates. Verify each price and inventory in Admin before publishing; do not advertise these as official Garena rates.
8. Test UID verification, product selection, order creation, UTR submission/review, tracking, admin login, image URLs, and mobile layout on the actual hosting environment.
9. Use only authorized Garena branding/assets and make clear whether the store is independent; do not imply official affiliation unless authorized.
